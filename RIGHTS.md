# Solarwave Rights Ledger

Snapshot date: 2026-10-29

## Current rights state

No third-party rights review has been completed for every future generated customer application. Rights therefore must be evaluated at the dependency/content level before any claim broader than the evidence supports.

## Current known boundaries

- The current Solarwave repository contains founder-directed project documentation and implementation created for this project.
- Generated applications should use standard-library or explicitly reviewed open-source dependencies only.
- Customer-provided names, logos, text, data, images, and trademarks remain subject to the customer's rights and authorization.
- Secrets, payment credentials, and API tokens are not rights assets and must not be committed to source.
- No claim is made here that every future generated application is automatically free of third-party license obligations.

## Commercial rule

Before delivery of a generated application:

1. Record dependencies and versions.
2. Record relevant licenses.
3. Record customer-supplied assets separately.
4. Record any assignment/license terms actually agreed with the buyer.
5. Do not represent a rights gap as a provenance gap.

## Ownership statement

Founder provenance is tracked in `PROVENANCE.md`. This rights ledger addresses chain-of-title and permitted-use questions only.
