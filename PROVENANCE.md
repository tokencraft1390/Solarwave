## Founder provenance

Founder-directed architecture and commercialization: Zachary Ribble.

Documented founder direction includes:

- The no-shared-boilerplate / fresh-generation concept.
- The single-file customer application constraint.
- The three-tier commercial packaging ($5 / $10 / $50).
- The Router -> Generator -> Validator/Linter -> Deployment Harness -> State model.
- The objective to make the system function as a vending-machine-style commercial workflow: order/payment -> generated app -> delivery/deployment -> evidence/receipt.
- The decision to keep customer applications isolated rather than sharing a customer codebase.
