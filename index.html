Re-quote / out of scope

We reject scope creep instead of hiding it.

  • Multiple roles or custom RBAC/auth
  • Regulated/highly sensitive data
  • Multiple integrations
  • Complex relational/reporting systems
  • Embedded payment processing
  • Ambiguous acceptance criteria
FAQ

What an agency needs to know.

Do I have to reveal Solarwave to my client?

No. The standard commercial model is white-label fulfillment. Your agency keeps the client relationship, branding and markup.

Does my client get the Solarwave factory?

No. Ordinary delivery can include the generated artifact/source if that is part of the accepted order. The core generator, orchestration logic, internal prompts and validation process remain outside the deliverable.

Is delivery guaranteed in a fixed number of minutes?

No. Standard accepted scopes target same-business-day delivery after complete inputs and verified payment. Internal production targets are not sold as guarantees.

What happens if my request is too large?

Solarwave returns OUT OF SCOPE and re-quotes it rather than silently expanding the job.

Can we start before payment clears?

Not for a new standard partner. Production begins after the accepted scope and payment state are verified.
