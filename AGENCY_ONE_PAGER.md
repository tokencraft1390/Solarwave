# Solarwave — Agency White-Label Micro-App Fulfillment

## The offer

You keep the client, your brand, and your markup. Send Solarwave a bounded requirement; Solarwave returns a tested buyer-specific micro-app you can deliver under your own client relationship.

This is not another SaaS subscription and it is not a replacement for your architecture team. It is an overflow production lane for the small custom surfaces that otherwise sit in backlog or consume expensive senior-builder time.

## Wholesale packages

| Package | Wholesale | Typical fit |
|---|---:|---|
| Quick Utility | $350 | Intake form, calculator, status page, small static utility |
| Working Micro-App | $650 | CRUD tracker, dashboard, portal-lite, admin utility |
| Connected Micro-App | $1,250 | Working micro-app plus one webhook/API integration or bounded trigger |

Rush lane: +$250 when explicitly accepted. Recurring care: $199/month for up to two bounded changes per month. First clean agency pilot: $350 when the job fits Quick Utility scope.

## What stays bounded

- Maximum five core user-defined fields.
- One role in the standard lane.
- One integration maximum in the Connected tier.
- Clear one-sentence acceptance test required.
- No regulated/highly sensitive data, custom RBAC, complex relational systems, embedded payments, or multi-integration builds without a separate quote.

If the requirement is outside the lane, Solarwave returns **OUT OF SCOPE** instead of silently expanding the build.

## How an agency orders

1. Send a sanitized client requirement: app name, outcome, fields, actions, integration need, and “done when” acceptance test.
2. Solarwave returns IN SCOPE / OUT OF SCOPE, fixed wholesale price, inclusions, and exclusions.
3. New partners prepay the accepted standard scope through an available payment/invoice rail.
4. Production begins only after payment is verified.
5. Solarwave generates, validates, tests, records an evidence receipt, and returns the accepted delivery package.
6. The agency delivers to its client and keeps the client relationship and markup.

Standard accepted scopes target same-business-day delivery. An internal production-time target is not a customer guarantee.

## Delivery boundary

The generated artifact/source may be included when specified in the accepted order. The Solarwave core factory, generator, validation/orchestration logic, internal prompts, and operating process are not transferred as part of an ordinary micro-app delivery.

## Buyer-safe proof

Ask for one real sanitized requirement instead of booking a generic demo. Solarwave will normalize the scope, show the applicable package, state what is and is not included, and—after payment—deliver against the acceptance test.
